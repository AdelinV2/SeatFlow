Demo and Test Mode

This site is a portfolio/demo deployment using Stripe Test Mode. Test payments, tax previews, and refunds move no real money. Nothing on this page is an offer of live payment processing, and no promise is made that demo tax values are suitable for real invoicing or accounting. If the deployment ever switches to live mode, this page becomes a mandatory release-review item and will be updated before any real-money use.

Currency and displayed prices

Seat prices carry their configured currency for display, and checkout shows the currency it will actually charge. The current payment implementation creates its Stripe payment intent in USD; where a seat tier is configured differently, the checkout total and the payment record are authoritative and this page states no conflicting currency promise. Amounts, tax preview figures, and payment states shown at checkout are informational demo values unless the operator confirms a live deployment.

Tax preview: inputs and outputs

During checkout you may enter a billing address to preview the tax included in the displayed total. The preview needs the street address, city, postal code, and country; an apartment or suite line and a state or province are optional where they apply. It answers one question only: of the tax-inclusive total already shown, how much is the tax portion and how much is the net portion, plus the effective rate Stripe Tax derived for that address.

The preview uses Stripe Tax in Test Mode with tax-inclusive behavior: it breaks the total down instead of adding an extra charge on top. Entering an address never charges anything by itself; it only asks Stripe Tax for that breakdown. A preview can fail (for example when the test gateway is unreachable); checkout then continues without a tax breakdown rather than inventing one.

What SeatFlow keeps

A payment record keeps the identifiers needed to reconcile the order (payment, reservation, event, and where present session linkage), the customer email linkage, the amount, the derived tax amount and net amount, the currency, the payment status with timestamps, and the Stripe payment-intent and integration identifiers stored server-side. Card numbers and CVC values are entered into Stripe Elements and are never stored by SeatFlow.

The billing address you type for a tax preview is transmitted for that preview; durable storage of the address beyond the payment tax and net record is not confirmed and is not claimed. How payment and address data is handled overall is also described in the privacy notice.

Which amount is final

A tax preview is informational: it does not lock the final amount. The charge is built from the held seat line items, and the total can still change before confirmation (for example when ticket types are updated), in which case the payment intent is refreshed and checkout shows the new authoritative total. The backend payment record and the provider confirmation decide what was actually charged; an earlier preview never overrides them.

Refunds and tax

The current refund scope is a full reservation refund, so a successful refund reverses the whole reservation amount including whatever tax portion was recorded for it. There is no separate tax-only or partial refund. The binding eligibility rule and workflow live in the refund and cancellation policy, which takes precedence over this summary.

No tax advice or invoice promise

SeatFlow is not a tax adviser and does not issue legally valid invoices on this demo. The preview categories above explain implemented behavior; they are not guidance on what tax applies to you and must not be used for accounting or filing. Card details are never stored by SeatFlow, and no internal provider identifiers, secrets, or request schemas are published on this page.
