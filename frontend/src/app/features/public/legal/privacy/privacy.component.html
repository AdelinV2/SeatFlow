Demo context

SeatFlow is currently a portfolio demonstration of event booking and ticketing. This notice describes the data flows implemented in the demo deployment. It is engineering transparency, not a lawyer-certified policy: before any commercial production use, the operator must confirm the controller identity, contact channel, hosting and provider configuration, and retention decisions listed below.

Controller and contact

The data controller and contact channel for privacy requests are not yet confirmed for a production deployment, so no company name, address, or data protection officer is stated here. Do not send passwords, payment secrets, or ticket QR codes by email when making a request. The contact route for requests is described on the contact page; no automated GDPR self-service portal exists in this phase.

Data categories

Depending on how you use SeatFlow, the demo processes:

  • Account and identity: user ID, email, display name, role assignments, OAuth provider identity where login is used, and account timestamps. No phone number is claimed as collected because the deployed UI and API audit did not verify it.
  • Guest and authenticated bookings: customer email and attendee name where supplied, optional linked user ID, reservation ID, event and session identifiers, selected seat IDs, price and tier selections, reservation status, hold expiry, and timestamps.
  • Payments and tax: payment and reservation identifiers, customer email linkage, amount, tax amount, net amount, currency, payment and refund status, timestamps, and Stripe payment-intent and integration identifiers persisted server-side. Card numbers and CVC are entered into Stripe Elements and are never stored by SeatFlow. Address fields typed for a tax preview are transmitted for that preview; durable persistence beyond the payment record is not confirmed and is not claimed.
  • Tickets: ticket code and ID, links to the reservation, payment, user, event, session, and seat, customer email, attendee name, display details, amounts, status, QR and ticket access data, and issuance, use, and cancellation timestamps.
  • Security and operations: transient rate-limit processing keyed by verified identity or client IP, request and correlation identifiers, application logs, traces and metrics, authentication and security events, and scanner validation records where identifiable. No durable IP-address storage is claimed; transient rate-limit keys are not the same as durable logs.
  • Assistant data (only where AI is enabled): the messages you send, the compact event, session, and seat context included to answer, and the in-memory conversation ID and owner metadata. Refreshing or signing out starts a new conversation; there is no persistent SeatFlow chat database in this phase, but prompts are still processed by the configured AI provider.

Browser storage behind these flows (authentication session, theme choice, guest checkout proof, Stripe payment state) is itemized on the cookies and similar technologies page.

Purposes and legal bases

Each purpose is mapped separately. Consent is not used as a blanket basis because no optional analytics, advertising, or profiling purpose exists in the verified runtime. Proposed bases below need legal confirmation before commercial use.

  • Provide sign-in sessions and account access: performance of the requested service.
  • Create, hold, price, and confirm guest or authenticated bookings: pre-contractual and contractual steps.
  • Process test or live payments and tax preview: contract, plus legal obligations for financial records where applicable.
  • Issue and validate tickets and QR access: contract.
  • Keep the service secure (rate limiting, fraud prevention, audit): legitimate interest, limited to deployed controls.
  • Remember an explicit theme choice: legitimate interest in honoring the setting.
  • Answer assistant questions where AI is enabled: performance of the requested assistance.

Recipients and providers

Providers are described as service providers or recipients because GDPR processor status is not confirmed for each deployment:

  • Supabase: authentication and account identity; project region and terms to be confirmed.
  • Stripe: payment processing, card UI, tax preview, fraud and security functions, webhooks; Test Mode applies to the portfolio demo.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: venue geocoding through direct browser requests, which disclose the typed query and network metadata to that endpoint.
  • Groq (only where AI is enabled): assistant model processing of prompts and tool context; provider retention and training policy to be confirmed for the deployed account.
  • Google Fonts: typeface delivery; page loads fetch stylesheets from Google Fonts infrastructure.
  • Hosting, email, and observability: the actual production providers are to be confirmed; local Docker topology is not presented as the deployed processing map.

International transfers

No transfer safeguard, adequacy claim, or residency guarantee (including any Romania or EU-only storage claim) is made here because provider regions and data-processing terms are not verified for each deployment. The operator must confirm Supabase, Stripe, Groq (where enabled), and hosting regions and terms before making any transfer statement.

Retention

  • Reservation holds expire automatically after 15 minutes.
  • Guest checkout email in the same browser tab ends with the tab session and is also cleared on cancellation, payment handoff, hold expiry, and stale-hold cleanup.
  • Theme choice persists in the browser until you change or clear it.
  • Authentication session copies follow the Supabase session lifetime and logout.
  • Assistant conversation state is bounded in memory and clears on refresh or sign-out.
  • Durable account, booking, payment, ticket, log, and metric retention periods are not stated as day counts because no automatic deletion rule is implemented for them in this phase. The operator must decide and document those periods before commercial use; until then, retention follows operational and legal necessity for a demo.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection (where applicable), and portability (where applicable). No processing purpose in the verified runtime relies on consent, so there is no consent withdrawal flow; if an optional purpose is added later, withdrawal will be offered at that time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Use the contact page for requests and identify yourself proportionately.

Is providing data required?

An email and attendee name are necessary to create the requested booking and deliver tickets; without them checkout cannot continue. Account creation data is necessary only if you choose to register. Payment data is necessary only if you proceed to pay. Optional analytics or marketing data is not requested because no such feature exists in the verified runtime.

Automated decisions

SeatFlow does not use automated decision-making or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. The AI assistant is assistive: it cannot approve bookings, set prices, or move money by itself.

Security

Authentication, role checks, server-side booking rules, Stripe-handled card entry, and rate limiting are summarized without sensitive detail on the security page.
