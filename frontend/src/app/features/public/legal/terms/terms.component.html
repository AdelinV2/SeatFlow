Portfolio demo nature

SeatFlow is a portfolio project that demonstrates event discovery, seat reservation, payment confirmation, and ticket delivery. There is no commercial operator identity stated on this page: no company name, registration number, VAT number, or headquarters is claimed. Before any real public or commercial deployment, the operator must confirm the controller identity, contact channel, jurisdiction, and whether the site remains a non-commercial demo.

Accounts and guest checkout

You may book as a registered user (email and password, or an enabled OAuth provider) or as a guest with a name and email address. Guest checkout stores your email in the same browser tab only, so you can continue that checkout; it is never added to the page address and is removed when the order is cancelled, paid, or the hold expires. Guest email is a convenience proof for that browser session, not a strong credential: keep your ticket link or code private instead.

How SeatFlow handles account, guest, and session data is described in the privacy notice and the cookies and similar technologies page.

Reservations and availability

Choosing seats creates a temporary hold of up to 10 seats per reservation for 15 minutes. Availability is decided by the server, not by what your browser last displayed: another customer may take the same seats first, and refreshing always reconciles against the authoritative state. When a hold expires or is cancelled, the seats are released for everyone else.

Payments and test mode

The public portfolio deployment uses Stripe Test Mode. Confirming payment with the provided test card completes a test transaction only: no real money moves. Card details are entered into Stripe Elements and are never stored by SeatFlow. Amounts, tax preview figures, and payment states shown at checkout are informational demo values unless the operator confirms a live deployment.

Tickets and QR access

Paid reservations produce tickets with a ticket code and QR access data. Anyone holding the ticket link or code can present it at the gate, so treat it like a paper ticket: do not post it publicly, forward it to strangers, or include it in support messages beyond what entry requires. Staff scanning validates the ticket code; SeatFlow does not replace the organizer's entry decision.

Refunds and cancellations

Cancelling a pending reservation releases the held seats. The binding refund and cancellation rules live in the dedicated refund and cancellation policy, which takes precedence over this summary. This page states no additional refund promise.

Acceptable use

Do not abuse reservations, scanning, APIs, or the AI assistant. This includes:

  • automated seat locking or denial-of-service behavior;
  • circumventing authentication, role checks, or ticket validation;
  • probing admin, scanner, or payment endpoints without authorization;
  • submitting unlawful, misleading, or infringing content through forms or the assistant.

Security controls that protect these flows are summarized on the security page.

AI assistant

Where the AI assistant is enabled, it is assistive only. It can explain events and propose bookings through the same server rules as the rest of the site, but it cannot bypass availability, holds, authorization, or payment confirmation, and it cannot create a payment on its own. Its answers may be incomplete: verify times, prices, and availability on the booking pages before paying.
