Event-specific support

SeatFlow does not operate a verified organizer-support directory: the application neither stores nor displays organizer contact information for events. Do not expect an organizer email, phone number, or support link here. For questions about a specific event (lineup, venue access, entry decisions), use the organizer channel stated on that event's official page, not this demo contact.

Privacy and data-rights requests

There is no automated data-rights portal and no data protection officer identity stated for this demo. To request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, or portability where applicable, use the maintainer channel above and identify yourself proportionately. How SeatFlow handles account, booking, payment, ticket, and browser-storage data is described in the privacy notice and the cookies and similar technologies page.

Security reports

To report a suspected vulnerability, use the maintainer channel above with a careful description of what you observed. Do not include passwords, payment secrets, authentication tokens, or ticket QR codes, and do not probe admin, scanner, or payment endpoints without authorization. High-level protections are summarized on the security page.

What to include

  • Which page you were on (for example the FAQ, an event page, or checkout).
  • What you tried and what you expected to happen.
  • For refund questions, the binding rule is the refund and cancellation policy.

Never include passwords, payment secrets, or ticket QR data beyond what entry requires. Related pages: frequently asked questions, privacy notice, platform status.
